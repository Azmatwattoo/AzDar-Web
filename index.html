Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information before starting your project.

How do I order a website?

Contact us through WhatsApp or the enquiry form. Tell us your business type, required pages, features and budget. We will discuss the project scope and quote.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the design, pages, features and platform. The prices on this page are starting estimates and must be confirmed for your specific project.

Will my website work on mobile?

The website designs offered here are planned to be responsive. Final testing should be completed on the agreed screen sizes before delivery.

Do domain and hosting come with the price?

Not automatically. Domain, hosting and third-party subscriptions may be separate costs. Confirm these details in the final quote.

Can I request custom features?

Yes. Describe the features you need. Login systems, databases, payments and AI integrations may require a backend and additional development costs.
